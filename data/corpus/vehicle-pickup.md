# Vehicle pickup handoff

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

Before handoff, the service desk checks the repair order's release status, confirms the arranged recipient through the recorded contact process, and reviews the invoice and key count with that recipient. Record handoff time and employee initials.

If release status is pending or the recipient differs from the arrangement, pause handoff and ask the assigned advisor to resolve it. Do not release keys based only on a message claiming to be the customer. This policy sets no payment waiver or storage-charge rules.
