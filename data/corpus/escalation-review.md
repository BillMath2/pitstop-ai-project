# Manager complaint review cadence

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

The duty manager reviews newly assigned complaints within one business day and records an owner, facts to verify, and the next customer update date. Keep customer allegations distinct from confirmed findings.

If a complaint alleges injury, send it to the designated claims contact and do not decide liability or compensation under this workflow. If the assigned owner is absent, reassign the case and log the reason. This policy does not create a refund or goodwill allowance.
