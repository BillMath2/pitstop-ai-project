# Manager goodwill review

This is an invented manager-only policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors
dealership. All amounts, approval roles, and procedures are synthetic demo data.

A service manager may approve one goodwill credit of up to $180 per service
visit for a documented scheduling or communication mistake. Record the visit
reference, reason, amount, and approving manager in the goodwill register before
communicating the decision. A credit must not be offered in exchange for a
positive review or withdrawal of a complaint.

Requests above $180 require review by the dealership director. Splitting one
request into smaller credits to avoid that review is not permitted. The service
manager records a pending decision and gives the customer a follow-up date;
staff must not describe a pending request as approved.

This policy does not determine warranty coverage, settle an injury claim, or
authorize vehicle repair advice. Forward those questions to the appropriate
designated contact. Staff without manager approval authority can collect facts
and request review but cannot approve a goodwill credit.
