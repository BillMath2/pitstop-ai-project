# Loaner vehicle return check-in

This is an invented operational policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors
dealership. It is demo data, not a real dealership procedure or vehicle safety advice.

When a customer returns a loaner, the service desk records the return time,
odometer reading, and key count in the loaner log. The employee walks around the
vehicle with the customer when the customer is available and notes visible
changes from the checkout record. Recording a condition does not determine who
caused it or authorize a charge.

The employee asks the customer to check the cabin for belongings and confirms
the customer's preferred contact method. Unclaimed belongings go into the
labeled lost-property cabinet; payment cards and identity documents go directly
to the duty manager.

If keys are missing or a condition differs from the checkout record, mark the
return as pending review and notify the duty manager. Do not promise a refund,
waiver, or repair completion time. The service desk may acknowledge the return
while the manager reviews the exception.
