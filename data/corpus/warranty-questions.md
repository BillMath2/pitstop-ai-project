# Warranty question intake

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

Record the customer's warranty question, vehicle details supplied by the customer, visit reference, and supporting documents offered. Send the question to the warranty coordinator for a coverage review.

Neither a customer report nor a public recall listing establishes warranty coverage. Staff must label coverage as unconfirmed until the coordinator provides a documented determination. This policy contains no manufacturer coverage periods, deductibles, or eligibility rules.
