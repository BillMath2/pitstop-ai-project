# Customer status updates

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

The assigned advisor provides an update by 3 p.m. on each business day while a vehicle remains checked in, even when no completion estimate is available. Use the recorded contact preference and log the time, channel, and factual status.

If the customer cannot be reached, leave only a callback request through the approved channel and record the attempt. Do not include detailed vehicle or payment information in voicemail. A parts arrival estimate is not a promised repair completion time.
