# Personal property handling

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

Record found belongings with the repair-order reference, discovery time, item description, and employee initials. Ordinary belongings go in the labeled lost-property cabinet. Payment cards and identity documents go directly to the duty manager.

The service desk arranges collection through the customer's recorded contact method and logs the handoff. Do not copy identity documents into general notes, disclose another customer's property, discard items, or promise shipping under this policy.
