# Estimate approval and scope changes

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

The advisor gives the customer an itemized estimate and records approval, date, time, contact channel, and the exact approved scope in the repair order. Silence is not approval. A previous visit's approval does not carry over to a new repair order.

If scope or estimated price changes, pause the additional work and obtain a revised approval before proceeding with that work. Record a declined item separately. This policy supplies no automatic dollar allowance for extra work and does not authorize staff to provide repair instructions.
