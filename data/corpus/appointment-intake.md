# Appointment intake

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

At booking, record the customer's concern in their own words, preferred contact method, requested date, and whether transportation assistance is requested. Repeat the concern back before saving the appointment. Keep a requested date separate from a confirmed slot.

The service desk confirms a slot only after checking the appointment calendar. An appointment reserves an intake time, not a diagnosis, repair completion time, loaner, or warranty decision. Route technical questions to the assigned advisor and do not infer a repair from a symptom.
