# Manager refund review

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

The service manager may approve an invoice correction refund up to $250 for a verified duplicate charge. Record the invoice reference, duplicate line, amount, reason, and approver in the refund register before directing processing to the cashier.

Amounts above $250 require dealership director approval. Do not split a refund to avoid review. A goodwill request without a duplicate charge follows the separate goodwill policy. A pending refund must not be described as processed or approved.
