# Open-job shift handoff

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

At shift change, the outgoing advisor lists open repair orders, current documented status, outstanding approvals, promised update times, and the next responsible employee in the handoff log. The incoming advisor acknowledges receipt.

Escalate an unassigned open job to the duty manager before leaving the desk. Distinguish confirmed facts from supplier estimates and customer requests. A handoff does not authorize extra work, extend an approval, or establish a completion promise.
