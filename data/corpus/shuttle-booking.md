# Shuttle booking

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

The fictional courtesy shuttle takes reservations for destinations within eight road miles of the dealership. The service desk records destination, passenger count, requested departure window, callback method, and any accommodation request for the coordinator.

The coordinator confirms capacity and pickup arrangements before a ride is promised. A request outside eight road miles goes to the coordinator for alternatives; it is not automatically approved. Do not promise a return ride until it is separately confirmed.
