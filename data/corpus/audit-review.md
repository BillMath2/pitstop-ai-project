# Manager adjustment audit

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

Each Friday, the service manager reviews that week's goodwill, refund, discount, and loaner-extension registers for missing approvers, split requests, and unresolved pending decisions. Record the review date, sampled visit references, findings, and follow-up owner in the audit log.

Escalate an unexplained adjustment to the dealership director. Preserve the original record and add a dated correction if needed. An audit finding does not retroactively approve an adjustment or authorize disclosure of manager-only records to other roles.
