# After-hours drop-off intake

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

The drop-off envelope records customer name, callback method, vehicle description, and requested concern. Customers place keys in the designated key slot and keep personal documents and payment cards out of the envelope.

At the next staffed opening, the service desk logs the keys and contacts the customer to confirm the request. An envelope is not repair authorization and does not establish diagnosis or completion time. Do not publish access codes or describe a vehicle as safe based on drop-off notes.
