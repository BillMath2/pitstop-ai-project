# Parts backorder communication

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

When a part is backordered, the parts desk records the part reference, supplier status, date checked, and next review date in the repair order. The advisor tells the customer that the arrival date is unconfirmed unless the supplier provides a dated estimate.

Review an open backorder every two business days. Label supplier estimates as estimates and log changes. Do not promise substitute-part suitability, free transportation, or a repair finish date; refer those requests to the advisor for review.
