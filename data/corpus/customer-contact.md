# Customer contact preferences

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

Record the customer's preferred contact channel and permitted callback number or address on the repair order. Confirm any changed contact details through the existing recorded contact process before sending visit information to the new destination.

Use contact details only for the current service communication described here. Do not send payment card details or identity-document copies through text or email. If a preference is absent, ask the customer; do not assume marketing consent from a service visit.
