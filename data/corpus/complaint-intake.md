# Complaint intake and escalation

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

Listen to the concern, record the customer's wording, visit reference, preferred callback method, and requested resolution in the complaint log. Send the entry to the duty manager before the end of the current staffed shift.

Acknowledge receipt and explain that a manager will review it. Intake staff cannot promise a refund, admit liability, approve a credit, or condition review on deleting a public complaint. If the duty manager is unavailable, assign the entry to the next duty manager and record that handoff.
