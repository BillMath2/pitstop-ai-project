# Loaner checkout record

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

The service desk confirms a reserved loaner and records checkout time, odometer, key count, visible condition, and the agreed return contact in the loaner log. Ask the customer to review the condition record before handing over the keys.

A service appointment alone does not reserve a loaner. If availability is uncertain, ask the transportation coordinator to confirm it. Exceptions to the recorded return arrangement go to the duty manager. This policy does not set eligibility, insurance terms, deposits, or charges.
