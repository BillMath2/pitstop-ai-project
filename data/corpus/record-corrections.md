# Repair-order record corrections

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

Correct a record by adding a dated amendment with the original entry reference, reason for correction, corrected information, and author initials. Keep the original entry visible; do not overwrite or backdate it.

Notify the assigned advisor when an amendment changes a customer-facing estimate or status. A correction does not itself grant approval for additional work or change a manager decision. Requests to erase an audit history go to the duty manager.
