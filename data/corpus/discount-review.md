# Manager labor discount review

This is an invented policy for the fictional Cedar Junction Motors dealership.
All procedures, limits, and roles are synthetic demo data.

The service manager may approve a scheduling-error labor discount of up to ten percent of the labor subtotal, capped at $120 per visit. Apply the smaller limit, record the reason and approving manager, and exclude parts and taxes from the discount base.

A larger adjustment requires dealership director approval. Do not stack this discount with a goodwill credit for the same scheduling error. Staff must receive approval before quoting the adjustment as final.
